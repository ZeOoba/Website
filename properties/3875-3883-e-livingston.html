About this property

3875-3883 E Livingston is a 10,950 SF industrial property in Columbus, Ohio, with a warehouse building and a separate office building on about 1.2 acres.

The warehouse has 22' clear height and two grade-level drive-in doors with drive-through capability. A paved, fenced yard provides outside storage.

  • 22' clear height
  • Paved, fenced yard with outside storage
  • Generator system and floor drains
