About the Portfolio: Common Questions

Oxford owns 513,000+ SF of shallow-bay industrial, flex and light industrial outdoor storage (IOS) space, plus a small share of retail and office, across Maryland, Columbus (Ohio) and Kansas City (Missouri).

Shallow-bay industrial buildings are small-format industrial and flex buildings, typically divided into multiple units that each have their own loading. In Oxford's portfolio, units generally run from about 1,500 to 15,000 SF with at-grade drive-in doors or truck-height docks.

Oxford is a family-owned firm that invests 100% proprietary capital and manages its properties as permanent holdings.

Current suites across the portfolio are listed on the Available Space page, where you can also start a tenant application.

Brokers and owners can submit an offering memorandum or rent roll and get a preliminary indication of interest within 1 business day. Oxford buys all-cash and closes in 30 to 45 days.
