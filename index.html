Sepich Portfolio

Columbus, OH · 110,000 SF · 35+ Tenants

Situation
35+ tenants on short-term leases at well-below-market rents, owned by the original builder’s heirs and managed remotely.
What we saw
Not a vacancy problem but a rent and marketing problem, in full buildings bought at a $42/SF basis.
What we did
Moved rents toward market gradually to avoid a vacancy spike, and used non-traditional marketing to reach more small-business tenants as units rolled.
Result
Units that rented for under $6 PSF gross now rent for $13 PSF gross. NOI has doubled since the February 2021 purchase.

3540 E. Fifth Avenue

Columbus, OH · 35,886 SF · Value-Add Repositioning

Situation
A vacant, functionally obsolete truck-repair facility near Columbus International Airport, bought for $1.3M in January 2022.
What we saw
A usable shell (rear and side loading, heavy power, 15’ to 19’ ceilings) whose problems were condition and environmental history, not location or function.
What we did
A ~$1.0M renovation (skin, roof, paving, doors, offices, HVAC) plus vapor mitigation systems, working with the Ohio EPA.
Result
A 10-year lease to a global fragrance manufacturer at ~12% yield on total project cost.

The Firm

A Family Office, Not a Fund

Founded in 2005, Oxford Realty Advisors is a principal investor and operator with 513,000+ SF of shallow-bay industrial and flex space across two core markets, plus a Kansas City holding — acquired with 100% family capital, no outside investors, and no fund structure.

We target the structural undersupply of functional Class B/C industrial space in secondary markets, acquiring below replacement cost and driving returns through active management. Conservative leverage means we can close all-cash, with no financing contingency.

Oxford acquires Class B/C shallow-bay industrial, flex, and IOS (industrial outdoor storage) properties under 50,000 SF per building, typically priced between $1M and $10M.

Oxford invests 100% family capital with no outside investors, no fund structure, and no investment committee. Oxford can close all-cash in 30–45 days with no financing contingency.

Oxford’s core markets are Maryland (DC Metro, Baltimore City, Eastern Shore) and Columbus OH. Oxford also holds a property in Kansas City, MO, and is actively evaluating expansion into additional Mid-Atlantic and Midwest markets.

Oxford is a permanent-capital buyer, not a fund with a defined exit timeline. Properties are acquired for generational, 40-year hold rather than a scheduled disposition.

Brokers, owners, and other intermediaries can submit deals directly through our broker portal. Every submission receives a preliminary indication of interest within 1 business day.
