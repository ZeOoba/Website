Structural Advantage

The Financing Gap Is Our Market

Most industrial properties in the $1M–$10M range are unfinanceable — not because they lack value, but because they fail a lender’s checklist. Significant vacancy eliminates DSCR coverage. Environmental conditions trigger lender exclusions. Deferred maintenance fails property condition minimums. Short lease terms don’t meet WALT requirements.

Our all-cash structure removes the lender entirely. We close deals that no financed buyer can.

Category Why Lenders Walk Why Oxford Closes
High Vacancy DSCR insufficient for loan sizing No minimum occupancy threshold
Environmental Conditions Lender exclusion lists; agency restrictions Phase I/II experience; we evaluate on merits
Deferred Maintenance Fails property condition report minimums CapEx priced into basis; no lender holdbacks
Short or M-T-M Leases WALT below lender minimums Cash flow risk absorbed in underwriting
Legal or Title Complexity Title insurer won’t clear; lender walks Counsel engaged early; complexity is pricing dislocation
