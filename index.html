The Firm

Oxford Realty Advisors

Founded in 2005, Oxford Realty Advisors is a principal investor and operator with 600,000+ SF of shallow-bay industrial and flex space across four core markets — acquired with 100% family capital, no outside investors, and no fund structure.

We target the structural undersupply of functional Class B/C industrial space in secondary markets, acquiring below replacement cost and driving returns through active management. A 15% portfolio LTV means all-cash closings on every deal.
